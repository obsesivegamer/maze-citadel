How it plays

Build the maze

Every tower is also a wall. Place them on the 20 × 28 tile plateau to stretch the route from the red portal to the blue gate. The glowing path redraws with every tower you place.

You can wall as hard as you like, but never shut the road: a tower that would seal the portal off from the gate turns red and won't build.

Read the wave

Three seconds before a wave spawns, its banner names what is coming: the creeps, their armor class and their element. Each element deals double damage to the next one around the ring.

Pierce beats Light armor and fliersSiege beats ArmoredPoison ignores armor

Hold the gate

You start with 20 lives. A creep that slips through costs one, a boss costs two, and the leaker loops back to the portal for another pass with no bounty.

Upgrade towers twice, then fuse a pair of maxed towers into an Epic such as the Frost Wyrm or the Doom Cannon. Clear wave 40 to win, or try Hard and Infinite. Your best wave in each mode is saved.

Controls

Keyboard first, mouse for the rest

1–0
Pick a tower, then click a tile. Shift-click keeps placing.
N
Call the next wave early
U
Upgrade the selected tower
Space
Pause. You can still build while paused.
X
Sell for 75%, or right-click
F
Cycle speed ×1, ×2, ×3
G
Fuse two maxed towers into an Epic
WASD
Pan. Scroll or pinch to zoom, Q E to rotate.

Install

Three steps to the citadel

  1. Open the disk image

    Double-click {{dmg_name}} in your Downloads folder.

  2. Drag Maze Citadel to Applications

    Or play it straight from the disk image. Nothing else to install.

  3. Double-click to play

    The game opens straight into the citadel with the builder ready.

  4. Allow it once in Privacy & Security

    Maze Citadel isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first double-click shows "Apple could not verify Maze Citadel is free of malware". Click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and click Open Anyway. Confirm, and from then on it opens with a double-click.

    On macOS 13 or 14 you can instead Control-click the app and choose Open.
