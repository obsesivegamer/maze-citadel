Build the maze

Every tower is also a wall

Towers block the tile they stand on, so where you build decides the demons' road. Stretch it and every tower gets longer to shoot. On the real 20 × 28 tile plateau the glowing path redraws with every tower you place.

Wall as hard as you like, but never shut the road: a tower that would cut the portal off from the gate won't build. Try it on this practice board.

Read the wave

Know what's coming, build what counters it

Three seconds before a wave spawns, its banner names what is coming: the creeps, their armor class and their element. Each element deals double damage to the next one around the ring and half to the one before it.

Keyboard first, mouse for the rest

Call the next wave early

Upgrade the selected tower

F
Cycle speed ×1, ×2, ×3
G
Fuse two maxed towers into an Epic
WASD
Pan. Scroll or pinch to zoom, Q E to rotate.

Install

Three steps to the citadel

  1. Open the disk image

    Double-click {{dmg_name}} in your Downloads folder.

  2. Drag Maze Citadel to Applications

    Or play it straight from the disk image. Nothing else to install.

  3. Double-click to play

    The game opens straight into the citadel with the builder ready.

  4. Allow it once in Privacy & Security

    Maze Citadel isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first double-click shows "Apple could not verify Maze Citadel is free of malware". Click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and click Open Anyway. Confirm, and from then on it opens with a double-click.

    On macOS 13 or 14 you can instead Control-click the app and choose Open.
